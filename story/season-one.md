# The Four Banners — Season One Story Bible

## Premise

For fifty years, the River Accord has kept four rival Houses from returning to civil war. Public Shobu duels settle claims between them. The player begins as Naya Aster, whose House was stripped of its banner after a devastating flood. She enters the Conclave to challenge the official account of what happened.

The central clue is in the flood-night ledger: it records a two-space setup move followed by a one-space aggressive move. Shobu requires the second move to match the first, so the recorded turn could not have happened.

The mystery is not simply who altered the record. Each House has a defensible reason to protect part of the truth, and publishing the full account could reopen the conflict the Accord was created to end.

## The Houses

- **Aster** — river engineers; disgraced after the flood. Naya seeks the truth about her sister Ena and the lost Aster banner.
- **Veyr** — guardians of the realm. Sol Veyr's brother's seal appears on the flood-night order.
- **Orme** — builders and smiths. Tavi Orme is Naya's first rival, a theatrical challenger who notices more than he lets on.
- **Merren** — grain merchants and negotiators. Their heir knows how every version of the record benefits a different House.

These identities affect dialogue, portraits, heraldry, and board trim. They never grant special moves or change Shobu rules.

## Season One Arc

1. **The Bannerless Challenger** — Naya duels Tavi, learns the Conclave's oath, and discovers the impossible ledger entry.
2. **The Brass Ledger** — a Merren heir offers a second copy of the record in exchange for a political favor.
3. **The Veyr Countermove** — Sol challenges Naya; his careful play and guarded testimony reveal that he is also searching for the truth.
4. **The Forfeited Board** — a retired champion admits she deliberately lost a territory to protect a witness.
5. **No Safe Alliance** — Naya and Sol trade evidence but each withholds a key piece.
6. **The House Without a Banner** — Tavi finds that tournament pairings have been manipulated to keep certain records sealed.
7. **Four Copies, Four Truths** — the archive copies disagree, and evidence implicates Aster's own leaders in the cover-up.
8. **The River Accord** — Naya faces Sol in the final. The player decides how much of the full record to publish and who must answer for the flood.

## Episode 1 — The Bannerless Challenger

### Opening

The Conclave courtyard is crowded. Four banners hang above four fixed boards. Naya stands beneath the empty bracket where the Aster banner used to be.

**Registrar:** “House Aster has no standing at the Conclave.”  
**Naya:** “Then enter me without a House.”  
**Tavi:** “That's allowed?”  
**Registrar:** “It is now. Orme will sponsor the challenge.”  
**Tavi:** “I was going to say something grand. That was grand enough.”

### Before the duel

**Tavi:** “One move to show your intent. The same move again to prove it. That's the Pact.”  
**Naya:** “And if I change my mind?”  
**Tavi:** “Then you'll have to change it on the board.”

### Duel

Naya plays Black against Tavi, who plays White. The match uses the standard Shobu rules. The first tutorial page explains the setup move; the next explains the matching aggressive move. A legal two-space diagonal opening earns the optional **Opening Oath** mastery seal.

Tavi's short reactions can fire at authored checkpoints, such as after the first push or the first ejection. His skill comes from the selected AI difficulty; his personality is expressed through writing and presentation, never rule advantages.

### Post-duel reveal

If Naya wins, Tavi slips her a bronze archive key. If she loses, he blocks the hecklers' view and offers to sponsor her next match after seeing that she read the board. Either outcome advances the story.

Naya compares the flood-night ledger with the rules: two-space setup, one-space aggressive move.

**Naya:** “The flood-night ledger records a two-space setup followed by a one-space aggressive move. That turn could not have happened.”

Sol Veyr steps out from behind the archive screen.

**Sol:** “My brother's seal is on that page. I don't know who put it there. Meet me at the next banner.”

The episode ends with the bracket updating to **Naya Aster vs. Sol Veyr**.

## Progression Principles

- A story loss changes the scene but does not block the campaign.
- Mastery seals unlock dossier pages, portraits, and cosmetic board themes; they never affect move legality or AI strength.
- Each duel should reveal character through both dialogue and a recognizable play pattern.
- Keep all story text in English to match the app interface.
